Unstir · the look of everything around the tank

Room to breathe: pick one of three directions

You said the text, buttons, cards and sheets around the tank are too busy and never let anything rest. Below are three ways to fix that, drawn on six real screens beside today's. The glass, pictures and covers are cut from real screenshots of today's build and are not touched. No text is smaller than today. Nothing in the app changes until you pick.

Still water

One thing in focus at a time. Today's daily, or the next level, is large and says "play ›"; everything else is one quiet line. Titles move to Apple's proportional face, numbers stay mono. Colour leaves the text and sits as a soft glow behind the one thing in focus. Things arrive by settling, like sediment; the result's dots drop in one by one.

build
about 3 days
risk
medium; a new type voice, and a rule for what is in focus
C

Mist

All the chrome turns white, with no outlines or lines. The colour moves out of the words into a large, blurred light that drifts slowly at the top of each screen and blooms once when a tank is solved.

build
about 3 days
risk
medium-high; the haze can look muddy, and it costs the phone's graphics chip while the glass plays

Why B

B is the only one that shows fewer things at once instead of restyling the same things. The menu leads with one action, the result card drops from four lines of text to two, and the play screen is the tank, its title and two words. Its motion is one slow settle everywhere, which reads as liquid rather than decoration. It keeps the neon identity where it counts: the wordmark, the covers, the glass, and a glow of the tier's colours behind the focus. Its glows are fixed images, so they cost the graphics chip nothing during play, unlike C's moving mist. A is safe but leaves the screens about as full as today.

If you do nothing, the chrome ships as it is today. The App Store screenshots and preview film are next in line after this pass, so they would be retaken on today's look.

A · Subtract

Motion stays as today: quick fades. Reduce Motion: as today.

What the player would see go or change
  • The tier buttons, "again", "up a notch", undo, reset, buy and "try 3 free" lose their outlined capsules and become words.
  • Every level row drops its second line ("3 rods · par 1"). The daily keeps its own.
  • The "today" heading, the divider lines and the dock's heading ("packs · paid add-ons", "first 3 levels of each free") go.
  • The archive's line shortens to "with any pack".
  • The shop card's "for / free / holds" labels and "after plughole · reading by colour" go.
  • Settings drops the explanation under every switch but colour letters.

B · Still water

Everything arrives on one slow curve, rising a few points out of a slight blur and stopping softly, over about a second. The glow behind the focus swells and shrinks over nine seconds. Reduce Motion: things fade in, the dots appear filled, the glow holds still.

What the player would see go or change
  • Everything in A's list, and:
  • Titles and buttons in SF Pro instead of SF Mono; numbers and counts stay mono.
  • The menu leads with one large item and "play ›": today's daily here, the next unplayed level when there is one. Endless and the archive share one line.
  • Level numbers lose their rainbow walk and glow; colour lives in the wordmark, the covers and the glow behind the focus.
  • The tier names lose their locks and dots; a locked tier still says why when opened.
  • The dock's names and "new" words go; a small dot marks a new pack.
  • In play the title is centred over the tank, larger. The "PRISM" tag over a Prism level goes.
  • The result: "unstirred." moves under the tank; "moves 9 · par 9" goes when the verdict is "at par"; "clean needs no undo or reset" waits behind a tap on the empty dot.
  • The shop card is centred, drops its "pack · paid add-on" label, shortens its facts, and its bundle line reads "all three packs, $5.99 ›" without the saving.
  • Settings drops every explanation line (VoiceOver still reads them); switches are white, not cyan.
Result marks and Prism's colour

The dots are white and drop in one at a time, each landing with a small squash. Prism's level number is white, split at its edges into red and blue the way a prism splits white light, so it never reads as one channel.

C · Mist

The light drifts on a 22-second loop, slower than the eye follows. Solving a tank blooms it brighter, then it settles. Reduce Motion: the light holds still at its settled brightness.

What the player would see go or change

  • All chrome text is white. The wordmark loses its colour sweep, level numbers lose their colours, and "at par", "clean" and "+2" lose amber, lime and magenta.
  • The dock's names and "new" words go; a small dot marks a new pack.
  • The "PRISM" tag over a Prism level goes.

Largest text

Text never shrinks

No direction sets any text smaller than today's size for its role: 17 pt for body text and buttons, 15 for labels, 13 for small print. Where space runs short in play, the tank scales down, as it does today.

What happens next

On your pick

The picked direction is built in the app, screen by screen, and every changed screen comes back to you as screenshots and films on the phone before it merges.

Mixing

You can take one direction and borrow a single piece of another (for example B with C's bloom on a solve). Say which piece.

Waiting on this
