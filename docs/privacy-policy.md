## In short

The game itself collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It talks only to
Apple's services: the App Store, through StoreKit, for prices and purchases, and, where a US state law asks, Apple's
age-range check. The one exception is this policy's web page, which the game loads when you open it from settings.
The only data we ever receive is an email you choose to send us and, if you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight,
what Apple passes on about your testing.

## What stays on your phone

The game keeps these on your phone only, and never sends them anywhere:
- Your progress: best scores, which levels you have started, the tier the menu shows, endless's best run, and a level
  you left unfinished, so you can carry on where you were.
- The undo bank and the day it last refilled. To keep the refill fair when the phone's clock is changed, the game also
  keeps the last time it trusted, the phone's time since it started up, and an identifier of that start-up that the
  system provides.
- Which lessons you have learnt (turning the tank, using the swirl), and which notes the game has already shown you,
  such as a new pack's mark or whether it has asked you for a rating.
- Your settings: music, sounds, haptics, colour letters and the supporter mark.
- Any Ask to Buy request made on this phone and when, so the game can say it is waiting for approval.
- How much drawing work the phone keeps up with smoothly, learnt as you play, so the pictures don't stutter, and
  pictures the game has drawn ahead, in its cache.
- A log of recent play, kept for finding bugs: its newest part, about half a megabyte to a megabyte, of where each
  touch landed and moved, each move, undo and reset, the levels and cards opened, whether you own a pack as its levels
  open, failed purchases and the App Store's errors, the settings you switch, when the app started, and similar notes
  of the game's state; a line about the phone (the app's version, the iOS version, the phone's model, its language and
  region, the text size, whether Reduce Motion, VoiceOver and Low Power Mode are on, and the game's own settings); and
  how warm the phone says it is and whether it is charging, noted at launch and whenever its warmth changes. Each line
  has the time on your phone. It holds nothing about who you are: no name, account or device identifier.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. If your phone backs up to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes it, under your
backup's own terms.

## Purchases

Packs and bundles of packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or
Apple ID. The game checks Apple's signed record of what you own on the phone, with no server of ours. To show prices
and know what you own, the game asks Apple's App Store when it starts and when you buy or restore. That is Apple's
service, and Apple's privacy policy covers it.

## Your age range

Where the law asks apps to check their players' ages (some US states), the game asks Apple for your age range once
each time it starts, and Apple may ask you whether to share it. Every level is for everyone, so the answer changes
nothing: the game keeps none of it and sends it nowhere. To tell us about anything in the game at odds with its age
rating, write to `{{CONTACT_ADDRESS}}`.

## If you report a problem

Settings has a "report a problem" button. It opens an email to us with the play log described above attached, and a
line in the message about the phone: the app's version, the iOS version, the phone's model, its language and region,
the text size, whether Reduce Motion, VoiceOver and Low Power Mode are on, the game's own settings, and how much drawing
work the game has learnt the phone keeps up with. You can read and
change the email, and it goes only if you send it. Where Apple's Mail app has no account on the phone, as when you use
another mail app, the button opens your phone's share sheet instead, with the log, our address and the same line, and
you choose where it goes. The game sends nothing by itself.

If you email it, or email us at all, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them
only to answer you and to fix the game. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you and keeping the
game working. You never have to send anything; if you don't, we can't look into the bug. Our email is held by
{{EMAIL_PROVIDER}} in {{EMAIL_PROVIDER_COUNTRY}}. Nobody else sees it, unless the law requires us to show it, for
example to the regulator. We delete the email and any log once the report is dealt with, and within 12 months of your
last message at the latest.

## If you test a beta

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple tells us how many sessions you played and on which days, and
passes on any feedback, screenshots or crash reports you send through TestFlight. Apple identifies you to us only by an
opaque tester id, or by the name and email you were invited under. We use this only to improve the game, keep it no
longer than 12 months, and never share it.

## Sharing a result

and resets, the supporter mark if you have bought something and left it on and, once the game has a link of its own, that
link. It goes only where you send it.
